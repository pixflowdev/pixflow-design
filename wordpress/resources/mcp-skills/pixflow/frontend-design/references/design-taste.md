# Design Taste — Pixflow adaptation

Consider purpose, audience and existing brand together. A portfolio can make room for expressive imagery; a service page may benefit more from a clear offer and accessible contact action. These are choices, not keyword-based presets.

## Composition and character

Give the page a recognisable visual idea grounded in its content: a photographic point of view, typographic rhythm, material detail or distinctive arrangement. Generic decoration rarely communicates what makes the business different. Novelty alone is not a reason to replace a familiar layout that serves the brief.

Create a clear reading order. Let emphasis, proximity and space explain which content belongs together. Repeated components establish consistency while different content types receive appropriate treatment. A portfolio, process and testimonial do not have to share the same card grid. Labels and badges should add information rather than fill a template.

For reference-site requests, study the rendered reference's scale, spacing, hierarchy, imagery and motion. Translate those relationships into the user's own content and identity. Describe any part you could not inspect rather than inventing evidence.

## Typography, colour and imagery

Choose type for the brand and reading task. Display text can be expressive while body copy, navigation and captions remain comfortable to read. Judge actual wrapping and glyph clearance, including italic descenders. Fonts and palettes are not banned by this reference.

Use colour relationships consistently across surfaces and interaction states. Existing tokens are meaningful starting points in site-style mode. In free style, a coherent reusable system can express the new direction. Neither mode requires a fixed number of accents or a universal light/dark aesthetic.

Choose images for their subject and crop, not just their colour. Supporting images should add information or demonstrate different work. Overlaid text needs a readable area at relevant crops; a solid-colour contrast calculation cannot prove readability over a photograph. A scrim, separate caption or changed crop may serve the composition better than text shadow.

## Responsive design and interaction

Recompose for narrow screens where needed: ordering, navigation, image crop and spacing can change while preserving the story. Keep captions and controls legible instead of shrinking everything to fit. Use the site's actual breakpoint system.

Motion can communicate a transition, relationship or response. Match its cost and intensity to the task; meaningful content and controls remain available without animation. Reduced-motion behaviour and keyboard focus are part of that design. Use existing native capabilities where they serve the result; this reference does not prescribe React, Tailwind, animation libraries or extra dependencies.

For redesigns, retain useful content, established identity and working interactions unless the brief calls for changing them. Spend effort where it improves understanding or the ability to act, rather than restyling every surface.
